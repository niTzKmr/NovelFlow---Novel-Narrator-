## 🌟 Why NovelFlow?

Cloud audiobook and text-to-speech services (like ElevenLabs or Speechify) charge expensive monthly subscriptions and strict monthly character caps. Narrating a single 100,000-word novel can cost \$40 to \$90 in API credits.

**NovelFlow runs 100% locally on your computer.**
- **Zero API Fees & Zero Subscriptions:** Unlimited listening forever with zero per-word costs.
- **100% Private & Offline:** Your books, reading progress, and generated audio never leave your computer.
- **Hardware-Accelerated:** Powered by Alibaba's open-source **Qwen3-TTS** with CUDA Graph acceleration on NVIDIA RTX GPUs, with automatic CPU fallback for standard laptops.

## ✨ Features

- 🎧 **High-Fidelity AI Narration:** Built-in cinematic, conversational, and expressive narrators.
